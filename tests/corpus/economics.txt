Economics - Supply and Demand
Objectives
Explain the law of supply and the law of demand
Identify what causes a shortage and a surplus
Demand is the quantity of a good that consumers are willing and able to buy at each price.
Supply is the quantity of a good that producers are willing to sell at each price.
The law of demand states that when price rises, quantity demanded falls.
The law of supply states that when price rises, quantity supplied rises.
Equilibrium is the point where quantity supplied equals quantity demanded.
A shortage happens when quantity demanded is greater than quantity supplied.
A surplus happens when quantity supplied is greater than quantity demanded.
Inflation is a general increase in prices over time.
Opportunity cost refers to the value of the next best alternative given up.
Factors of production:
- Land
- Labor
- Capital
- Entrepreneurship
Scarcity means that resources are limited while human wants are unlimited.
A price ceiling is a legal maximum price set by the government.
