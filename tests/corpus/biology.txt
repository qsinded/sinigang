The cell
The nucleus stores the cell's DNA and controls its activities.
Mitochondria are organelles that produce most of the cell's ATP.
Ribosomes build proteins from amino acids.
The cell membrane controls what enters and leaves the cell.
Chloroplasts are organelles in plant cells where photosynthesis takes place.
Photosynthesis is the process by which plants turn light, water and carbon dioxide into sugar and oxygen.
Plant cells have a cell wall because the wall gives the cell a fixed shape and extra support.

Types of cells:
• Prokaryotic cells
• Eukaryotic cells
• Plant cells
• Animal cells

Enzymes are proteins that speed up chemical reactions.
Osmosis is the movement of water across a membrane from a dilute to a concentrated solution.
